          About this prototype

          Adoption Signal Radar: DAP Market Intelligence Prototype, by Sriram Sudarsi (PGP-SM, IIM Lucknow).

          Why I built this

          I wanted to understand the digital adoption platform (DAP) market: how big it is, which enterprises need adoption help right now, how they decide to buy adoption tools, and how a DAP business could reach the right people.

          The tool asks four questions a founder's office would ask: where is demand showing up, why now, who matters, and what should we do next? It answers them from public, sourced signals: system migrations (above all SAP ECC to S/4HANA), AI rollouts to employees, mergers, new tech leaders and visible adoption pain.

          An independent student project. Not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any company named here.

          What's real and what's simulated

          How the score works

          Score out of 100 = fit (up to 20: US headquarters, sector with large DAP spend, known size) + triggers (up to 50: migration, AI rollout, merger, new leader) + pain (up to 30: ERP problems, adoption gaps, go-live within six months, hiring and more). Signals older than six months count 0.6×, older than a year 0.3×; indirect evidence is discounted. Strong = 65+, or a trigger plus pain at 50+. Moderate = 40+. Scores are computed as of the data snapshot so they don't drift.

          The weights are my judgment, not fitted to real win/loss data. Validating them is on the roadmap.

          How it was built

          • AI-assisted research to find candidate articles, then I opened and checked each source before it went in. Anything I couldn't verify went to a watchlist or was dropped.
          • Every signal carries its publisher, date, link and a trust note (primary source, trade press, vendor case study…).
          • One self-contained HTML file: no server, no tracking, no API keys. The AI reader uses the host's built-in AI only if the page runs somewhere that provides it; otherwise keyword rules.
          • People: executives appear only if named in public news or press releases. Personal and alumni connections are shown by role only.

          Roadmap

          Today

          Articles are verified and added by hand. The AI reader analyses pasted articles. Refresh is simulated.

          Next

          AI scans news on a schedule and updates signals automatically, with a person approving new sources.

          Later

          CRM integration, and validation of scores and recommendations with real companies, if it proves useful.
